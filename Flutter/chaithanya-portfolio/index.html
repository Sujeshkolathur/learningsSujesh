About

Precision modeling with a construction-first mindset

Hi, I'm Chaithanya — a BIM designer focused on architectural and multidisciplinary coordination. I work across residential towers, commercial campuses, and healthcare facilities, ensuring models are accurate, federated, and ready for site teams.

My process blends design intent with buildability: structured families, clean parameters, clash detection workflows, and documentation that reduces RFIs on site.
